Claude plans have a usage limit that resets on a schedule. When a reply runs into it, the work stops. ZumiBot does not leave the chat stuck: it tells you when the limit resets and continues by itself.

<Shot name="chatting-limit-wait" alt="A chat where the agent's reply was cut off. Under it is the line 'Usage limit reached · continues today at' and a time, with two buttons, 'Continue now' and 'Don't continue'." />

## What you see

When the limit stops a reply, the chat shows a line instead of a red error:

**Usage limit reached · continues today at 22:21**

The time is in your own time zone. Two buttons sit under it:

- **Continue now** sends the continue right away, in case the limit is already lifted.
- **Don't continue** cancels the wait. Nothing is sent by itself.

In a group room, the line names the member the limit stopped, such as "Researcher reached the usage limit".

## What happens by itself

About a minute after the reset, ZumiBot sends the message "continue" to the agent in the same chat, so it picks the job up where it left off. In a room, each member the limit stopped gets its own "@name continue".

ZumiBot has to be running at that time. If you restart it in the meantime, it sets the waits again, and one that is already due goes out at once.

## Messages you queued

Messages you sent while the agent was busy wait in the **Tasks** list. They are not sent into the limit one after another. They go in after the continue, oldest first.

If you write a new message while the chat waits, it goes in straight away and the wait ends. Choosing **Send now** on a waiting message does the same.

## When it does not continue by itself

Some stops cannot be waited out. The line then reads "Usage limit reached · resets today at 22:21", or just "Usage limit reached":

- ZumiBot does not know when the limit resets.
- Another error came with the limit.
- It already continued 5 times in a row and hit the limit again each time.

Nothing is sent, and your waiting messages are held in **Tasks**. When you are ready, send a message yourself, or choose **Send now** on a held one.

## Tips and limits

- **Don't continue** also holds your waiting messages. Choose **Send now** on one to send it.
- A paused chat does not wait. A limit that ends a pausing reply leaves the chat paused, and only **Resume** continues it. See [Pause and Resume](/chatting/pause-resume/).
- Any message of yours, an edit, a resend or a version switch ends a wait.
- A Compact that the limit stopped does not start a wait.
- A chat in which another agent asked this one a question hands the limit back to that agent as the answer, so it does not wait.
