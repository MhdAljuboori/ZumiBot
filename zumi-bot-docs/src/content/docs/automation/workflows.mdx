A workflow is a chain of steps you draw on a canvas. A trigger starts it. Each step does one job, such as asking an agent to write something, asking you to approve it, or sending you a notification. The output of one step feeds the next.

A [routine](/automation/routines/) sends one prompt. Reach for a workflow when the job has several parts, needs your approval in the middle, or has to choose between paths.

## Build your first workflow

This one drafts release notes with an agent, waits for your approval, then tells you it is done.

<Steps>

1. **Open Workflows** in the left bar and choose **+** (New workflow). A new workflow has one box, the **Trigger**, and is Off. Type its name at the top.

2. **Add a step.** Choose **Add step** in the toolbar at the bottom of the canvas, or press N, and pick **Agent**. The new step lands in the middle of the view.

3. **Join it to the trigger.** Drag from the Trigger's right-hand dot to the step. A drag that ends on empty canvas opens the same menu and joins the step it adds. A dot with nothing after it shows a small **+** that adds a step there.

4. **Fill it in.** Choose the step. Its settings open in the panel on the right. For an Agent step you pick the agent, a folder and the prompt. [Steps](/automation/workflow-steps/) covers every kind.

5. **Pass text along.** In a prompt, type `{{` to pick what to insert: `input` is the text the previous step handed on, and another step's output or a trigger field are in the list too. A step's **Handle** names it for this, so a step with the handle `draft` is read as `{{steps.draft.output}}`.

6. **Add the next steps.** Add an **Approval** step and a **Notify** step the same way. On Approval, join the **Approved** port to the Notify step. If you leave the **Rejected** port empty, nothing runs after a rejection.

7. **Check and save.** With nothing selected, the right panel says "No problems: it is ready to run." when the workflow is complete. Otherwise it lists each problem, and choosing one opens that step. Choose **Save**, or press Ctrl+S.

8. **Run it.** Choose **Run workflow**. A box asks for an optional **Input**, which becomes the trigger's text. Choose **Run**. A line reads "Run started." with **View run**.

</Steps>

A new workflow is saved Off. **Save** always works, but **Run workflow** and turning the switch On wait until the workflow has no problems. If you save a workflow you asked to be On and it has problems, ZumiBot keeps it Off and says why.

## The Off and On switch

The switch matters for workflows that start by themselves: a schedule, a webhook or an event. An Off workflow never starts on its own, but you can still run it by hand. In the editor, the switch is saved with your other changes. The switch in the Workflows list applies at once.

## Work on the canvas

The toolbar at the bottom of the canvas has four tools: **Select**, **Pan** (or hold Space), **Add sticky note** and **Add step**. The buttons at the lower left zoom in, zoom out, fit the view and reset it to 100%.

| To do this | Do this |
| --- | --- |
| Move a step | Drag it. Steps snap to a grid. |
| Pick several | Drag a box on empty canvas, Ctrl-click (Cmd-click on a Mac) each one, or press Ctrl+A. They move, copy and delete together. |
| Join two steps | Drag from one step's right-hand dot to the other step. |
| Put a step on a line | Point at the line and choose its **+**. The trash removes the line. |
| Open a step | Choose it, or press Enter. Press F2 to rename it. |
| Copy and paste | Ctrl+C, Ctrl+X and Ctrl+V work between workflows too. Ctrl+D duplicates. The trigger cannot be copied or deleted. |
| Undo and redo | Ctrl+Z and Ctrl+Shift+Z (or Ctrl+Y). ZumiBot keeps your last 100 edits. |
| Zoom | Ctrl and the wheel, or the + and - keys. Press 1 to fit the view and 0 to go to 100%. |
| More | Right-click a step, a line or the empty canvas for a menu with each action and its shortcut. |

Two helps for big workflows are for people, not for the run:

- **Sticky notes.** Press Shift+S, or choose **Add sticky note**, and write in Markdown. Pick a color from the bar that shows when you point at the note. Notes sit under the steps and the workflow never reads them.
- **Groups.** Pick two or more steps that form one connected chain and press Ctrl+G, or use the right-click menu's **Group**. A group is a named box. Collapse it to one block to tidy the view. The steps in it still run. Ctrl+Shift+G ungroups. A group must be one unbroken chain with one way in and one way out, and the editor says why when yours is not.

## Run it and read the result

A run lists under its workflow in the Workflows list, with a dot for its status, the time, a tag such as "manual", and what it cost. Choose a run to open it. A run's status is one of "Running", "Waiting on you", "Done", "Failed", "Stopped" or "Skipped".

<Shot name="automation-workflow-run" alt="The read-only canvas of a run, with the same four steps. 'Trigger' and 'Draft the notes' have a green tick. 'Approve the notes' has an amber pause mark and a bright outline. 'Tell me it is done' has no mark yet." />

The run shows your canvas with a mark on each step: a tick for done, a pause for waiting, a cross for failed. Choose a step to see each time it ran, called a visit, with its input, its output, how long it took and what it cost. Two things need you:

- **An Approval step waits.** The run status reads "Waiting on you", and the Workflows icon in the left bar shows a dot. The question is in the step's panel, with **Approve** and **Reject**. The same card shows in your [Inbox](/automation/inbox/).
- **A step failed.** Choose it and press **Retry** to run that step again with the same input.

The header has **Canvas** and **Audit log** tabs, and a **Stop** button while the run goes. **Stop** ends the run at once: it stops the agents' turns, cancels calls in progress and closes open approvals. The Audit log appears once the run has ended. It lists the trigger, then every visit in order with what went in and what came out, and ends with how the run finished, how long it took and what it cost.

Each Agent step runs in a chat of its own, titled with the workflow, the step and the date. Choose **Open chat** on a visit to read it or to answer a card the agent raised. The chat is archived when the step finishes, unless you chose to keep it.

## Start a workflow from a chat

Type `/run-workflow` in the message box of an open chat, pick a workflow from the list, and add your text after its name. That text becomes the trigger's text, and the chat's title and the agent's last reply there are available as trigger fields. A notice reads "Started" with **Open run**. See [Slash commands](/chatting/slash-commands/).

## Tips and limits

- **Only five at a time.** A workflow can have five unfinished runs.
- **Loops are allowed, up to a point.** You can join a step back to an earlier one. A step may run 20 times in one run, and a run 200 visits in all. After that the visit fails with "Ran too many times", and **Retry** allows more.
- **A run does not change when you edit.** A run keeps a copy of the workflow as it was when the run began.
- **Run settings.** Select the trigger to find **At the usage limit**, **If a run was missed** (schedules) and **Spend cap per run**, which work like a routine's. See [Triggers](/automation/workflow-triggers/).
- **On a phone** you can turn a workflow On or Off, run it, and read its runs. The note "Change steps on a larger screen." means you edit steps on a computer or tablet.
- **The ⋯ menu.** **Duplicate** makes an Off copy named like "Release notes (2)" with the same steps. **History…** shows your saved versions, see [Saved history](/automation/saved-history/). **Discard changes** goes back to the saved version. **Delete** stops unfinished runs and removes the workflow and its runs. The chats it made stay.
- **Zumi Bot can build one.** Describe the flow to [Zumi Bot](/agents/zumi-bot/), or ask it to turn a chat into a workflow. It never runs the workflow itself.
- **Zumi Bot cannot be a step.** An Agent step needs one of your own agents.
