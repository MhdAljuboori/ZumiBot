When you have many chats, three things keep them in order. **Search** finds a message wherever it was written. **Unread** marks the chats that have a reply you have not seen. **Archive** tucks away chats you are done with, and keeps them safe.

## Search

<Shot name="chatting-search" alt="The left column with the search box open and the word 'checkout' typed. Under the two chats whose names match is a Messages section that reads '4 messages' and lists the hits. Each hit shows the chat's name, the time, who wrote it, and a snippet with the word checkout highlighted." />

<Steps>

1. **Choose the magnifier** at the top of the left column, which reads "Search agents, chats and messages". You can also press Ctrl+K (Cmd+K on a Mac) to go anywhere, and type there: the first few matching messages show under the places.

2. **Type what you remember.** Agents and chats whose names match show first. Under them, a **Messages** section lists the messages that contain your words, newest first. A line over the list says how many it found, such as "4 messages".

3. **Choose a hit.** The chat opens at that message, the message flashes, and your words are highlighted in it. Choose **Show more** at the end of the list to load the next 30.

</Steps>

Each hit shows the chat's name, an **Archived** tag if it is archived, when it was written, who wrote it ("You" or the agent's name), and a snippet with your words marked.

### How matching works

- Every word you type must be in the message. Up to 8 words count.
- A word of two letters or more matches the start of words, so "check" finds "checkout". Capital letters and accents do not matter.
- Put words in quotes to find them together in that order, such as `"release notes"`.
- Search looks at your messages and the agents' replies. It skips tool steps, thoughts, notices, approval cards, side questions and other versions of an edited message.
- Archived chats are searched too.

A message you just sent or received can take a couple of seconds to show up. After ZumiBot restarts, it reads your chats again in the background. The line over the hits then adds "still indexing older chats…", and the list fills in as it goes. If a search fails, the line reads "Couldn't search messages."

## Unread

<Shot name="chatting-unread-menu" alt="The left column with the Code Reviewer's chats listed. One chat, 'Write release notes for 0.4', has a blue dot at the end of its row. A menu is open on it: Rename, Export…, Make repeatable, Continue in Telegram, Continue in Slack, Archive and Delete." />

When an agent's reply arrives and you are not looking at that chat, the chat gets a blue dot in the left column. The dot also shows on the agent when its chats are folded, on a folded section, on the left column's button on a phone, and on the **Agents** button in the narrow column at the window's edge. Opening the chat clears it.

When you open a chat with unread replies, a **New** line marks the first message you have not seen, and the chat opens there.

An approval card that needs you shows a badge in the same place, and the badge wins over the dot.

### Mark a reply unread

Point at an agent's reply and choose **Mark unread**. The chat shows its dot again and a **New** line above that reply, and you stay where you are. It stays unread until you open the chat again.

## Archive

Archive moves a chat out of your list without deleting it. Use it for chats you might want later but do not want to see every day.

<Steps>

1. **Right-click the chat** or room in the left column (or press and hold on a phone).

2. **Choose Archive.**

</Steps>

A chat that is still running cannot be archived. The menu entry is grayed out and its tooltip reads "A chat can be archived once its reply ends".

### Find archived chats

<Shot name="chatting-archived" alt="The Archived list in the left column. It shows a 'Back to chats' link, the title 'Archived' with a count of 2, a box that reads 'Search archived', and two archived chats, each with its agent, folder and 'Archived today'." />

Choose **Archived** in the narrow column at the window's edge. The left column becomes the list of archived chats, with a search box that reads "Search archived". Choose **Back to chats** at the top to return to the normal list. Choose an archived chat to read it. A note on top reads "Archived. Sending a message brings it back." with an **Unarchive** button.

- To bring one back, choose **Unarchive** in its note, or in the menu of its row.
- To act on several at once, tick the boxes beside the rows. Shift-click ticks a range. A bar shows "3 selected", **Select all**, **Unarchive** and **Delete**.
- **Delete** asks you to confirm. It removes the chat from ZumiBot. Claude's own session file is left alone.

## Tips and limits

- Sending a message to an archived chat brings it back. So does a routine run into it, a question from another agent, or a Compact. That way no card that needs your answer hides in the archive.
- Archiving does not change the order of the rest of your chats. A room goes back to its old spot when you unarchive it.
- Unread marks and archive status are saved on the server, so every device you use shows the same.
